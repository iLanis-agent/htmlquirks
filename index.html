Your HTML is not what you wrote. It is what the parser built.

HTMLQuirks runs your markup through the browser's real HTML5 parser and shows the tree it actually constructed - plus every spec rule that silently rewrote your source along the way.

Your table's text moved house

Text inside <table> but before the first row can't live there - the parser "foster parents" it out, in front of the table. And that <tbody> you never wrote? It's there. table > tr matches nothing.

Paragraphs that end themselves

A <p> cannot contain a <div> - the parser closes it early and your layout breaks two elements later. A stray </p> with no opening tag doesn't vanish either: it creates an empty phantom paragraph.

Three kinds of text

<script> is rawtext: <p> inside it is just characters. <title> is RCDATA: entities decode but tags don't parse. Everything else parses fully. Same angle brackets, three different rules.
